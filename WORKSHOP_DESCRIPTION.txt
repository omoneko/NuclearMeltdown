[h1]Nuclear Meltdown[/h1]
When a nuclear power plant is destroyed by fire or a disaster, it doesn't just quietly collapse — it can trigger a catastrophic [b]meltdown[/b]: a huge explosion that craters the ground and flattens the surrounding district, followed by long-lasting radioactive ground contamination.

[h2]What happens[/h2]
Rolled randomly each time a plant is destroyed:
[list]
[*][b]5%[/b] — Scale 10.0 meltdown: enormous blast + crater + fallout over a huge area
[*][b]15%[/b] — Scale 5.5 meltdown
[*][b]45%[/b] — Scale 1.0 meltdown (base)
[*][b]30%[/b] — no blast, contamination only
[*][b]5%[/b] — collapse only (no blast, no contamination)
[/list]
The blast forms a crater and destroys nearby buildings, roads and trees (scaled by severity). Contamination reuses the game's ground-pollution system and is kept active by the mod so it does not fade away on its own.

[h2]Cleaning it up[/h2]
[list]
[*]Contamination clears on its own after [b]50 in-game years[/b], OR
[*]Build a [b]Decontamination facility[/b] (a building asset whose name contains "Decontamination") near the contaminated area — it gradually cleans fallout at about [b]5% per in-game month[/b].
[/list]
[b]Note:[/b] a Water Treatment Plant does NOT decontaminate — you need a dedicated Decontamination facility.

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Only plants destroyed by [b]fire or disaster[/b] trigger a meltdown — bulldozing a plant yourself does nothing.
[*]Explosions destroy surrounding buildings and can chain-react between nuclear plants placed close together.
[*]Save / load is supported — contamination persists across saves.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Required[/b]: Harmony (Mod Dependency) — you must subscribe to it, or this mod will not work.
[*][b]Optional[/b]: Natural Disasters DLC adds the meteor explosion visual/audio. The mod works fully without it (crater, destruction and contamination still happen).
[/list]

Feedback and bug reports are welcome!


=== 補足（日本語・任意で説明文の末尾に追記可） ===

[h2]日本語[/h2]
原子力発電所が火災や災害で破壊されると、確率でメルトダウン（大爆発＋クレーター＋周囲の建物破壊＋放射能汚染）が発生します。汚染はゲーム内50年経過、または「[b]Decontamination facility[/b]」（名前に "Decontamination" を含む建物）を汚染地域の近くに建てると約5%/ゲーム内月で軽減します（※下水処理場では除染されません）。手動での解体では何も起きません。Harmony (Mod Dependency) が必須。Natural Disasters DLC があると爆発の視覚/音効果が付きます（無くても動作）。
