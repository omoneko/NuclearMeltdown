Nuclear Meltdown

When a nuclear power plant is destroyed by fire or a disaster, it doesn't just quietly collapse - it can trigger a catastrophic meltdown: a huge explosion that craters the ground and flattens the surrounding district, followed by long-lasting radioactive ground contamination.

What happens (rolled randomly each time a plant is destroyed):
- 5%  - Scale 10.0 meltdown: enormous blast + crater + fallout over a huge area
- 15% - Scale 5.5 meltdown
- 45% - Scale 1.0 meltdown (base)
- 30% - no blast, contamination only
- 5%  - collapse only (no blast, no contamination)

The blast forms a crater and destroys nearby buildings, roads and trees (scaled by severity). Contamination reuses the game's ground-pollution system and is kept active by the mod so it does not fade away on its own.

Cleaning it up:
- Contamination clears after 50 in-game years, OR
- Run a Water Treatment Plant inside the contaminated area to gradually decontaminate it.

Only plants destroyed by FIRE or DISASTER trigger a meltdown - bulldozing a plant yourself does nothing.

REQUIRED:
- Harmony (Mod Dependency) - you must subscribe to it, or this mod will not work.

OPTIONAL:
- Natural Disasters DLC adds the meteor explosion visual/audio. The mod works fully without it (crater, destruction and contamination still happen).

Warnings:
- Explosions destroy surrounding buildings and can chain-react between nuclear plants placed close together.
- Save/load is supported - contamination persists across saves.

Feedback and bug reports are welcome!


=== 補足（日本語・任意で追記） ===
原子力発電所が火災や災害で破壊されると、確率でメルトダウン（大爆発＋クレーター＋周囲の建物破壊＋放射能汚染）が発生します。汚染はゲーム内50年経過、または下水処理施設の稼働で消えます。手動での解体では何も起きません。
※Harmony (Mod Dependency) が必須。Natural Disasters DLC があると爆発の視覚効果が付きます（無くても動作）。
